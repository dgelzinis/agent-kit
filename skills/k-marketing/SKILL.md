# /k-marketing — the visibility expert, four modes

> **Project settings first:** if the project's `AGENTS.md` has `## Skill settings` → `### /k-marketing`, read it and apply it on top of this skill (audience, positioning, target markets and languages, the project's SEO/GEO strategy document, channels in use, analytics access). Settings add steps or tighten rules; they never loosen the kit's Safety rules.

**Always start here:** read `~/GIT/agent-kit/stacks/seo-geo.md` (the standards and what is known to work), the project's own marketing or SEO strategy document if it has one, and its voice (`/k-content` writes the words; `/k-design` shapes the pages).

## Pick the mode
| The owner wants… | Mode | Steps in |
|---|---|---|
| to know how findable the site or a page is today, and what to fix | **audit** | `modes/audit.md` |
| a direction: who to reach, which questions and searches to win, which pages and channels, in what order | **plan** | `modes/plan.md` |
| one page created or improved so it wins a specific search or question | **page** | `modes/page.md` |
| to know what is working: traffic, rankings, AI mentions, conversions | **measure** | `modes/measure.md` |

- `/k-marketing audit …`, `plan …`, `page …`, `measure …` force a mode. Unsure → ask one question.
- Read **only** the chosen mode's file, in this skill's folder (`~/GIT/agent-kit/skills/k-marketing/modes/<mode>.md`), and follow it.

## In every mode
- **Honest marketing only.** No invented numbers, customers, reviews, testimonials, awards or comparisons. No claim the product cannot back. No fake accounts, paid-for or planted mentions, link schemes, hidden text or keyword stuffing. These are also what search engines penalise.
- **Search for current facts; do not rely on memory.** Search behaviour, competitors and the engines' own guidance change month to month. Use web search for keywords, competitors and current guidance, and say what you found and where.
- **People first.** A page earns visibility by being the most useful answer for a real person's question. Write for that person; structure it so engines and AI systems can read it.
- **Regulated topics** (money, health, law): no advice, no promises of outcomes, claims sourced; follow the project's rules.
- **You propose; the flow builds.** Findings and plans become tasks (`/k-task`); pages are built through the normal flow with `/k-content`, `/k-design` and `/k-code`. Never publish, post, email or submit anything outside the repo yourself; give the owner the text and the steps.
- **Say what cannot be known.** No one can promise a ranking or an AI citation. Give expected direction and how it will be measured, not guarantees.
