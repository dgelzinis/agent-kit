# Mode: measure — what is actually working

Needs data. Use what the project has (search console exports, analytics, a connected tool); if you have no access, say exactly which export or screenshot the owner should provide. Never estimate numbers.

1. **What to look at**, against the plan's baseline:
   - **Search:** impressions, clicks, average position and click-through, by page and by query; which pages gained or lost; indexing problems.
   - **AI answers:** referral visits from AI assistants (segment those referrers in analytics); the engines' own reports where they exist; and a re-run of the question panel from the audit (who is cited, who is named).
   - **On the site:** visits by landing page, what people do next, sign-ups or other conversions by source. A page that ranks but nobody acts on is not working.
   - **Beyond the site:** new mentions, links and listings since last time.
2. **Read it honestly.** Separate signal from noise (small numbers, seasonality, one-off spikes). Say what changed, the most likely reason, and how sure you are. Do not credit a change to an action without evidence.
3. **Decide.** For each thing tried: keep, improve, or stop. Then the next three actions, most valuable first, each as a task (`/k-task`).
4. **Record it.** A short dated note where the project keeps its plan: the numbers, what was learned, what is next. That note is the next measurement's baseline.
