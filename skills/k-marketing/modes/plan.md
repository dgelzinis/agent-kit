# Mode: plan — who to reach, what to win, in what order

Output: a short plan the owner approves, then a list of tasks. Save it where the project keeps its strategy (or propose `docs/brand/marketing-plan.md`).

1. **Start from the product and its people.** Who it is for (one or two specific audiences), the problem it solves for them, and what makes it different, in their words. Use the project's positioning and strategy documents; ask only for what is missing.
2. **Find the demand** (web search; say what you found and where):
   - the questions and searches these people really use: problems, "how to", comparisons, alternatives, pricing, the category name;
   - what currently answers them, in search results and in AI answers, and how good those answers are;
   - where these people already talk and look for recommendations (communities, review sites, newsletters, video).
3. **Choose where to win.** A handful of topics where the product has something true and specific to say and the existing answers are weak. For each: the intent, the page that should answer it (existing or new), and why this product is a credible answer.
4. **Pages.** The core set first (home, one page per audience or use case, pricing, comparison or alternatives pages where honest, a few genuinely useful guides or tools). Each with its target question, its promise, and its proof.
5. **Proof and original material.** What the product can show that nobody else can: real screens, real numbers, a small study, a calculator, a worked example. This is what gets linked to and cited.
6. **Presence beyond the site.** Where the product should genuinely appear: the directories and comparison sites of its category, communities where the owner can take part as themselves, partners, press. List the specific places and what the owner would do there. Nothing planted or paid-for-as-organic.
7. **Channels and rhythm.** Only what one owner can sustain: e.g. one useful page a fortnight, one post a week, replies in two communities. Fewer, kept up, beats many abandoned.
8. **How it will be measured.** The numbers (see `modes/measure.md`), the baseline today, and when to look again.
9. **Present it** as checkpoint 2: audiences, topics to win, pages in order, off-site actions, rhythm, measures. After the OK, write the plan to the project and offer the first tasks with `/k-task`.
